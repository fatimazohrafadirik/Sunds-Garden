ABOUT STOCKHOLM

Stockholm is the capital of Sweden and is built across fourteen islands. The city is famous for its colorful buildings, beautiful waterways and Scandinavian design.

Visitors can explore historic streets, interesting museums, peaceful parks and lively cafés.

THINGS TO DO

  • Explore the old streets of Gamla Stan
  • Visit the Vasa Museum
  • Take a boat tour around the islands
  • Try Swedish cinnamon buns
  • Walk through the Royal Palace

YOUR THREE-DAY PLAN

  1. Day One
    Explore Gamla Stan and visit the Royal Palace.

  2. Day Two
    Visit the Vasa Museum and explore Djurgården.

  3. Day Three
    Take a boat tour and enjoy a Swedish fika.
